Find them through Facebook birding groups, local naturalist networks, and Indian birders already visiting Kumaon — not international ads. The price of the trip is a review with full name and country, and permission to use your photos of them. That is your entire trust section, earned.

WEEK 3 ON

The index already lists 32 species; only Spotted Forktail has a page behind it. Write the rest in the order people search for them, and link each into the index as it lands. It is the reason to come here rather than anywhere else in the Himalaya, and the reason this is the tour I'd book first. Here is where I'd set expectations, in order.

Cheer Pheasant Catreus wallichii VULNERABLE
Why people come to Ranikhet

Global range is a thin band of the western Himalaya, and the grass-and-scrub slopes around Ranikhet hold one of the few dependable populations. It is the one bird I will not oversell: the dawn call carries a long way, the sighting is often brief, and some mornings give you nothing. We look for it on one morning of a three-day trip, and get a second attempt on the four.

The sanctuary

Binsar is about walking in true Himalayan sanctuary wilderness

Come here for the forest rather than for a target bird. Binsar is an Important Bird Area with 173 recorded species, but the reason to spend the nights inside is that there is nothing else inside — no traffic, no village, no other groups at dawn. The birds are what happens while you are standing in it.

What the two nights actually buy

Unbroken oak and rhododendron from the door of the rest house, first light without a drive, and the Zero Point ridge with snow peaks along the horizon on a clear morning. Nobody staying outside the gate gets any of that. In exchange the rest house is basic — cold water some mornings, simple food, no phone signal worth the name.

Spotted Forktail

Enicurus maculatus
SPFOR Resident Year-round · easiest in winter 1,200–2,400m

A stream bird, and one of the most dependable good sightings on the route. Where the pheasants need luck and a pre-dawn climb, forktails work a fixed stretch of water and come back to the same boulders every morning.

When is the best time to see a Spotted Forktail near Ranikhet?

Year-round, though winter is easiest — birds drop to streams around 1,400–1,900m and concentrate on fewer stretches of water. Early morning and the last hour of light are best, when they feed along wet boulders. There is no narrow seasonal window here, which is why it is a good bird to build a trip around if your dates are fixed.

Where to look

Fast, shaded streams with exposed boulders and leaf litter along the edge. The Chaubatia stream below the orchards is the reliable site near Ranikhet; the crossings inside Binsar hold a pair or two. Walk the water slowly and stop often — they flush ahead of you along the channel and settle again fifty metres on.

How to identify one

Black and white, with a white forecrown, white-spotted black mantle, and a long deeply forked tail banded in white that it fans and pumps constantly. Slaty-backed Forktail is plain slate above with no spotting. Little Forktail is much smaller, with a short tail and a restless bobbing action.

How to tell these are mine

You should not have to take my word for it, and there is no certificate for this sort of thing. What there is, is evidence you can check in about two minutes.

01
The originals still carry their EXIF

Camera body, lens, shutter, ISO, date and time are written into every file. Ask for any frame at full size and read it yourself. Stock libraries strip that data, and a lifted image will show someone else\u2019s camera or nothing at all.

02
One camera, one set of hills

The whole collection comes off the same body and lens, frame numbers running in sequence, every location within a few kilometres of Majkhali. A set assembled from other people\u2019s work never looks like that.

03
A public trail that predates this site

Most of these went up on Instagram as they were taken, dated. Reverse image search any of them — they should lead back to me and nowhere else.

04
The ordinary frames are here too

A bought portfolio is all hero shots. Mine keeps the flat light, the distant birds and the half-obscured ones, because that is what these forests actually give you.
