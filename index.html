Who I am

I'm Vivasvat. I've spent years birding the Kumaon region — the ridges above Ranikhet, the Binsar sanctuary, the streams. I guide from Majkhali now.

I run these trips myself, one group at a time, which is why there are never more than six of you and why the person answering your email is the person who'll be on the trail with you. Our base is Firwood Cottage in Majkhali — early starts, straight into good habitat, no long drive before the day begins.

What I do know is the ground: which forest road is worth walking after rain, where the forktails sit on the Chaubatia stream, and which slope has held birds recently. Every photograph on this site is mine, taken on these routes, with the date and the place on it.

Questions worth asking before you book

Plain answers, including where the trip has limits.

Why book with you rather than a larger operator?
Because I'm based here, at a small cottage near Ranikhet, and I walk these ridges whether or not anyone has booked. You get the person who knows which slope held birds last week, not a driver and a checklist. Groups never exceed six, and everything on this site is checkable — the exact route, the lodges by name, my own photographs, and a price with nothing hidden.
How do I get to Ranikhet from outside India?
Fly into Delhi, then either a short flight to Pantnagar and a 3-hour drive, or the overnight train to Kathgodam and a 3-hour drive. I meet you at either and the transfer is included both ways.
Do I need to be fit?
3 to 5km a day on graded forest road, with one gentle climb. No camping, nothing above 2,450m. If you can walk a few kilometres on uneven ground you will be fine — and tell me at enquiry if anything worries you.
What if only one person books?
Then the trip runs with one person, at the same price. Groups are capped at six and this season most will be smaller.
What does it cost, all in?
The published price covers every land cost: rooms, all meals, vehicle, guiding, permits and transfers. On top you need flights, an India e-visa of about $25, insurance, and a single supplement if you want your own room.

Tell me your dates and I'll tell you what's realistic

Including when the answer is that you should come in a different month. I'd rather lose the booking than have you fly here in June.
